About the Seminar

We focus on problems related to, but not limited to, portfolio optimization, autoformalization of statistics, high-dimensional data, and financial risk theory.

We hold a weekly online seminar across multiple universities, with informal lectures, and conduct collaborative research among faculty, industry researchers, and students.
